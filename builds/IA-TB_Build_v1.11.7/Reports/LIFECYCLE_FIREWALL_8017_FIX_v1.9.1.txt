IA-TB v1.9.1 — Lifecycle Fix

Root cause:
stop.command and start.command pre-cleanup did not recognize the health marker "IA-TB Firewall Service". Port 8017 therefore remained occupied even though the service exposed its PID.

Fix:
- Firewall marker recognition in start.command and stop.command.
- Graceful TERM + bounded wait for port release.
- Force kill only for a formally identified IA-TB service.
- Residual lsof cleanup only after service identity has been established.
- No UI changes.
